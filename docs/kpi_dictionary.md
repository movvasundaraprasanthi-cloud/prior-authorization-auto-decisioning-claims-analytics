# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Why Cohere Cares |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-decision rate | Authorizations completed through the auto-decision path divided by total authorizations | Measures automation performance and client value |
| Decision SLA met rate | Authorizations decided within client SLA hours | Tracks operational performance and patient/provider experience |
| Avoidable manual reviews | Manual reviews where policy match, documentation, and eligibility signals were clean | Identifies optimization opportunities for rule refinement |
| Average decision hours | Average elapsed hours from request intake to decision | Measures turnaround time and operational efficiency |
| Claims denial rate | Denied claims divided by total claims | Connects authorization strategy to downstream claims outcomes |
| Denied amount | Allowed amount associated with denied claims | Quantifies financial exposure and payment integrity opportunity |
| Estimated admin savings | Auto decisions multiplied by estimated manual-vs-auto review cost difference | Shows business value for clients and internal stakeholders |
| Client value opportunity | Avoidable manual review opportunity plus denial opportunity estimate | Prioritizes client optimization roadmap |
| Policy match rate | Percent of authorizations matching clinical policy logic | Guides clinical decision rule tuning |
| Documentation complete rate | Percent of authorizations with complete required documentation | Identifies documentation prompts and provider education needs |
